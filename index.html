About the Cebu-Bohol Educational Tour

The Cebu-Bohol educational tour was the best experience as an IT student. It allowed me to see real-world applications of the technologies we study in the classroom. I also gained valuable insights from industry professionals who shared their expertise. Beyond learning, the trip created unforgettable memories with classmates. We also had the chance to explore the rich culture of Cebu and Bohol, which made the experience even more meaningful.

November 19

Mactan Shrine

In Mactan Shrine, the history of the Philippines began to take shape through the legendary Battle of Mactan, located in Lapu-Lapu City. It commemorates the bravery of Datu Lapu-Lapu, who resisted Spanish colonization by defeating Ferdinand Magellan in 1521. This site now serves as a symbol of Filipino courage and independence. Many visitors come each year to honor the nation’s early struggle for freedom.

WorldTech Information Solutions, Inc.

From my observation, the facilitator, Ma’am Elizabeth, is a knowledgeable cybersecurity professional who is passionate about her work. She presented the cybersecurity orientation very effectively, showing both expertise and dedication. Although their company is small, they provide cybersecurity services to large organizations, including well-known banks and schools. I learned that every time you go online, your information can be compromised because the internet was not originally designed for privacy. This makes cybersecurity crucial in protecting and securing personal and organizational data.

November 20

CodeChum

They started at a very young age, just like us, when they created CodeChum by Sir Xavier and other creator. It was also clear that they were not afraid to experiment and learn from their mistakes. From this, I learned that every project or thesis we work on can spark an idea and lead to a passion for what we create. I also realized that success often comes from taking small steps and improving continuously. Most importantly, I learned that believing in your ideas can open opportunities you never expected.

CIT-U Wildcat Innovation Labs

Cebu Institute of Technology – University is highly recognized for its STEM education in Cebu. Their passing rate is 100%, reflecting the strong academic foundation they provide. This reputation continues to attract students who aim to excel in technical and scientific fields.

Rivan IT Cebu

Rivan IT Cebu provides extensive hands-on training using real networking and security equipment, including Cisco routers, switches, firewalls, and other modern devices essential for cybersecurity and IT infrastructure learning. Our instructor was very well-prepared and interactive during the orientation. I learned how the operations and systems work for transmitting analog voice signals over IP, and we also completed a practical activity, which was really fun. It also gave me an idea of where to train for certifications if I choose a network-related path, similar to WorldTech Information Solutions, Inc.

Magellan's Cross and Santo Niño

Magellan’s Cross and the Santo Niño de Cebu are among the most significant historical and religious symbols from the Spanish era in Cebu, representing the arrival of Christianity in the Philippines and the early interactions between Spaniards and native Filipinos.

November 21

Mata Technologies, Inc.

Mata Technologies, Inc. appears to be a highly advanced tech company, offering impressive visual and 360° previews of tourist attractions and hidden gems in the Visayas region, as well as detailed interior and exterior views of real estate properties. Seeing their work gave me more ideas on how to operate in a similar way, and it inspired me to explore more advanced techniques to enhance my own work. Their innovative approach showed me the importance of using modern tools to create engaging content. It also motivated me to continue improving my skills to keep up with evolving technology.

November 22

T.A.R.S.I.E.R. 117 (Telephone And Radio System Integrated Emergency Response)

TARSIER 117 operates by providing quick assistance during critical situations, and their coordinated, systematic, and well-organized approach ensures that emergencies are handled efficiently and effectively. They shared that they were inspired by Davao’s responsive emergency and disaster management unit, which was very insightful for me. I learned how important it is to respond quickly and stay organized during emergencies, and I also realized how teamwork and coordination play a major role in saving lives. As an IT student, I was inspired by how technology can support disaster management, from communication systems to data tracking.

Chocolate Hills View Point

Chocolate Hills is one of the natural wonders of Asia and country’s third national geological monument. During the dry season, the hills turn brown or “chocolate-colored.” When we visited the Chocolate Hills, they were green, but it was still a gorgeous sight. The view from the viewing deck is very beautiful once you reach it.

Bohol Tarsier Conservation Area

The Bohol Tarsier Conservation Area is dedicated to protecting the Philippine tarsier, one of the smallest primates in the world and a species native to the Philippines. Because tarsiers are highly sensitive creatures, the area provides a safe and quiet environment where they can live, feed, and reproduce naturally. It was also my first time seeing a tarsier.

Bilar Man-Made Forest

The Bilar Man-Made Forest is a two-kilometer stretch of densely planted mahogany trees and one of Bohol’s most iconic and visually striking attractions. It was created as part of a reforestation project to restore the environment and prevent soil erosion in the area.

Loboc River Cruise

Loboc River Cruise is one of the most relaxing and enjoyable experiences in Bohol. It takes visitors on a peaceful boat ride along the emerald-green Loboc River, surrounded by lush tropical scenery, with live music, cultural performances, and traditional Filipino songs. You can enjoy their tasty food and experience their rich culture. The song “Balik sa Bohol” is a remarkable piece that makes you want to visit Bohol again because of its beautiful and vibrant culture.

Sikatuna Mirror of The World

Sikatuna Mirror of the World is a theme park featuring miniature and life-sized replicas of famous landmarks from around the world. It offers a fun, educational, and visually appealing experience.

Baclayon Church

Baclayon Church is one of the most historically and culturally significant sites in Bohol. Officially known as the Church of Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception, it is one of the oldest churches in the Philippines, built in 1596 by Spanish missionaries. The church houses religious artifacts, centuries-old statues, and antique paintings that showcase the country’s rich religious history.
